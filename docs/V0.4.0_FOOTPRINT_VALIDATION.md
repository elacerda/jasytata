# Jasytata v0.4.0 footprint validation — Gate 3

## Scope and decision

Gate 3 validates the existing Schema v2 footprint shapes in their local east/north
tangent plane. It does not add a geometry family, change footprint roles, change
pointing lattices, or alter the published T80/S-PLUS geometry contract.

Regular hexagonal planning envelopes are ordinary polygon footprints with six
ordered vertices. No persistent `hexagon` type or production hexagon constructor
was justified: the polygon engine already supplies containment, area, bounds,
rotation, intersection and display boundaries. A hexagonal footprint says
nothing about the lattice of pointing centers.

For an ideal regular hexagon, this document names dimensions explicitly:

- **Circumradius** `R`: center to vertex.
- **Inradius** `r = R cos(30°)`: center to flat.
- **Vertex-to-vertex diameter** `2R`.
- **Flat-to-flat diameter** `2r = √3 R`.
- **Orientation** is geometric: *vertex on north* means one vertex lies on the
  local north axis; *flat normal on north* means the north-facing flat is
  perpendicular to that axis. These labels do not specify a camera PA or a
  pointing-center lattice.

Never store a regular-hexagon dimension as an unqualified `diameter`.

## Geometry audit

All local dimensions and areas below are in degrees and square degrees.

| Operation | Existing contract and Gate 3 result |
| --- | --- |
| Rectangle area and containment | `width × height`; inclusive edges and vertices. PA-zero containment preserves exact comparisons; rotated comparisons allow `1e-12°` for transform roundoff. |
| Circle area and containment | `πr²` and radial distance; inclusive edge uses the existing `1e-12°` allowance. Neither operation uses rendered samples. |
| Polygon area and containment | Absolute shoelace area; winding does not change area or interior. Simple concave polygons are supported. Edge and vertex tests use the existing local tolerance. The v2 validator rejects repeated vertices, zero area and self-intersections. |
| Compound area and containment | Containment is the union of nonrecursive child shapes. Area uses deterministic adaptive integration to depth 12, giving boundary cells no wider than `1/4096` of the local bounding-box scale; it is approximate and avoids summing overlapping child areas. |
| Local bounds | Primitive rectangle, circle and polygon bounds include their local PA. Compound bounds include transformed child offsets and rotations. Circle extrema are analytic. |
| Boundary paths | Rectangle/polygon paths are their transformed physical vertices. Circle paths are display-only circumscribed tangent polygons, with exact cardinal extrema. At the default 96 sides a circle path has 96 tangent sides, four inserted cardinal points and one repeated closing point. |
| Region intersection | Reports positive-area overlap only; edge or vertex contact alone is false. Polygon/rectangle topology uses local boundaries. Circle intersection uses strict containment and exact center-to-edge distance, never the display polygon. The existing geometry tolerance is `1e-12°` (with edge-orientation tolerance scaled by segment length). A projected region vertex must clear that margin before it counts as strictly inside, preventing sky-to-local roundoff from turning edge contact into overlap. |

Astronomical PA is east of north. For local `[east, north]` and angle `θ`,
rotation is `[east cos θ + north sin θ, north cos θ − east sin θ]`; therefore a
positive quarter-turn moves north toward east. Compound offsets rotate with the
parent, and component/child rotations compose with the parent angle.

### Circle display correction

The previous sampled circle path put all sample vertices on the analytic circle,
so its straight chords were inscribed and slightly under-drew the nominal radius.
Gate 3 changes only that display path to a deterministic circumscribed polygon:
each side is tangent to the analytic circle, and four exact radius extrema are
included along those sides. The default 96 tangent sides overstate the radius
only between samples; the axis-aligned bounds remain exactly `[-r, r]`.

The renderer consumes this conservative path through `tileFootprintBoundaries`.
Area, point containment and circle/region intersection remain analytic. A
regression fixture places a tiny region around a display vertex outside the true
circle and verifies that it does not count as scientific intersection.

The same region-intersection path now requires signed clearance greater than
`1e-12°` before a projected region vertex counts as strictly interior. A
regression at positive and negative declination showed sky/local roundoff could
otherwise move an exact edge contact a few ulps into an axis-aligned rectangle.
This intersection-only check leaves ordinary footprint containment and the
T80/S-PLUS coverage path unchanged.

### Sky projection boundary

The sky adapter retains the project's documented linear tangent approximation:
east is wrapped `ΔRA × max(cos(DEC), 0.01)` and north is `ΔDEC`. Region vertices
are projected into the pointing's frame before local intersection. Tests cover
RA wrap at 0°/360° and declinations from −60° through +65°. This is not spherical
polygon projection or spherical area; distortion grows for large footprints and
near the poles where the RA cosine is clamped. Gate 3 does not replace that
published model.

## Independent regression references

The fixtures use explicit arithmetic expectations; none calls the production
area, rotation or vertex-construction logic to calculate its expected values.

| Geometry | Independent checks |
| --- | --- |
| Non-square rectangle | A `4° × 2°` rectangle has area `8 deg²`; checks its center, four edges/corners, just-outside points, exact vertices/bounds at 0°, +90°, −90° and 180° PA, PA sign, and positive-area versus edge-only contact. |
| Circle | Radius `2°` has area `4π deg²`; radial/edge inclusion and outside rejection are analytic. The rendered path is deterministic, tangent on every side, contains all four exact extrema, and has analytic bounds. Tangency is not positive-area overlap. |
| Concave polygon | An explicit `4° × 4°` square with a `2° × 1°` notch has shoelace area `14 deg²`; clockwise reversal preserves area. Tests cover center, edge/vertex, notch and outside points, PA bounds, positive overlap and boundary-only contact. |
| Compound | Independent child corners check parent offset/PA plus child rotation. Tests preserve a central gap, retain disjoint components, and check overlap as a union rather than a sum, including duplicate children. |
| Regular hexagons | Static vertex-on-north and flat-normal-on-north polygons verify all six radii/sides, opposite-vertex symmetry, center, flats, vertices, both diameters, analytic area, PA rotation/bounds, boundary and intersection. They pass only through ordinary polygon engine operations. |
| Sky mapping | Independent tangent projection fixtures check both RA-wrap directions, positive/negative and substantially different declinations, a rotated boundary after projection, and an intersecting region crossing RA zero. |

`frontend/src/profiles/schema-v2.test.ts` also confirms that concave simple polygons
validate, self-intersecting polygons are rejected, and Schema v2 does not accept
a separate `hexagon` footprint type.

## Empirical fixture and unresolved boundaries

The one selected instrument fixture with a defensible hexagon dimension convention
is the nominal **PFS target-access envelope**. The [official PFS parameter table](https://pfs.naoj.org/research/parameters.html)
specifies an approximately `1.38°` hexagonal field diameter measured by its
circumscribed circle, plus an approximately `1.25 deg²` field area. The fixture
therefore names `circumscribedDiameterDeg = 1.38` and `circumradiusDeg = 0.69`;
it does not interpret that value as flat-to-flat width. An ideal regular polygon
at this approximate radius has flat-to-flat diameter `√3 R ≈ 1.195°` and area
`(3√3/2)R² ≈ 1.237 deg²`. The published area is only a rounded consistency
check. The fixture validates an approximate nominal target-access envelope, not
the distorted physical boundary, target assignment, or observed-area coverage.
Its vertex-on-north test orientation is a local mathematical convention, not an
assertion about PFS camera PA.

No exact regular-hexagon fixture is frozen for these other selected cases:

- **CALIFA/PPAK:** the published approximate `74″ × 64″` extents do not define
  exact regular vertices; do not reverse-engineer them.
- **MaNGA:** the selected 19-fibre mode's quoted `12″` nominal diameter does not
  establish its outer-boundary or diameter convention.
- **LVM:** its nominal half-degree hexagon does not establish the diameter
  convention or physical camera orientation.
- **4MOST:** the rounded nominal `2.5°` diameter and `4.2 deg²` area do not
  resolve flat-to-flat versus circumscribed diameter or the usable patrol edge.

Those physical-boundary questions remain profile-fidelity limitations for Gate 6.
They are not geometry defects and are not replaced by invented regular vertices.
The full evidence context remains in
[`V0.4.0_INSTRUMENT_MATRIX.md`](V0.4.0_INSTRUMENT_MATRIX.md).
