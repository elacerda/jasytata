## Placement architecture and mathematical contract

| Question | Gate 4 finding |
| --- | --- |
| Persisted placement | A Schema v2 survey stores `tiling.type`, two `basis_deg` east/north degree vectors, and `origin` (`region_center` or an ICRS `fixed_anchor`). Its separate inference and coverage policies are also persisted. Instrument footprint shape and profile PA are independent instrument data. |
| Runtime placement | Generic inference retains the projection reference, rotated basis when allowed, local phase offset, sky anchor, integer assignments, residuals, and diagnostics. Candidate indices and proposal metadata are runtime results; they are not written into a survey profile. |
| Catalogue inference | Generic inference aligns the declared fundamental basis to eligible dataset centers, optionally rotating it. It does not discover an arbitrary basis. Legacy S-PLUS uses distinct nonrotating, row-grouped spacing and per-row phase inference. `InferenceRole` controls evidence eligibility, while actual centers still contribute to coverage and occupancy. |
| Phase | `region_center` puts site `(0,0)` at the midpoint of continuous unwrapped RA bounds and DEC bounds. `fixed_anchor` puts `(0,0)` exactly at its declared ICRS coordinate with zero runtime translation. Successful region-local inference represents phase as an east/north `phase_offset_deg` in the original projection plane. |
| Sky projection | `footprint-engine.ts` converts east/north degrees to sky at the planning reference, dividing east by `max(cos(reference DEC), 0.01)`, adding north to DEC, and wrapping RA into `[0,360)`. Its inverse uses the same local reference. Candidate intersection evaluates the footprint around each sky center. |
| Footprint coupling | Basis orientation neither reads nor sets footprint PA. Candidate range uses a footprint's rotated reach only to find potentially intersecting sites; positive-area footprint/region intersection filters those sites. No generic path requires a rectangular footprint or orthogonal basis. |
| Determinism | Integer enumeration is `j` ascending, then `i` ascending. Range bounds come from the inverse basis applied to expanded local corners with one integer of padding. Occupancy preserves candidate order; deterministic greedy gain ranking selects the plan. Repeated identical requests return identical indices, candidates, and selected order. |
| Compatibility split | `planRegion` dispatches registered generic `lattice` to `planDeclaredLattice`. Registered `legacy_splus` enters `planLegacyRegion`, where supplemental filling is disabled for that survey type. Only inline v1 `RECT_GRID_V1` reaches the historical supplemental gap-fill branch. It cannot leak through the registered v2 or generic declared dispatch. |

For matrix columns `b1=(e1,n1)` and `b2=(e2,n2)`, a raw integer site is
`P(i,j)=O+i*b1+j*b2`. Its determinant `e1*n2-e2*n1` must be nonzero; Schema v2
rejects numerically degenerate normalized determinants at `1e-12`. A triangular
arrangement is ordinary `b1=(s,0), b2=(s/2,sqrt(3)*s/2)`. There is no
`HEX_GRID_V1`, shape-derived placement, overlap parameter in the basis, or
lattice-derived camera PA.

Three levels must stay distinct:

1. **Raw sites** are integer coordinates and sky centers from basis, origin,
   optional runtime alignment, and an index domain. They do not depend on shape.
2. **Admissible planner candidates** are raw sites whose specific footprint
   intersects the selected region with positive area and whose centers are not
   occupied by an actual pointing. Their membership can depend on footprint.
3. **Selected proposals** are greedily chosen candidates with useful new sampled
   coverage. Their number and order can differ across footprints and between
   Complete and Efficient. A center outside the polygon can be admissible when
   its footprint intersects the polygon.

## Independent fixtures and results

`frontend/src/science/gate4-lattice-validation.test.ts` computes expected local
coordinates and sky positions with explicit arithmetic, separate from the
production `latticePoint` and sky-conversion helpers. For edge completeness it
enumerates a wider finite integer domain with independently computed centers,
then uses the separately validated Gate 3 footprint/region intersection to
identify useful sites. The fixture covers:

| Case | Independent expectation and result |
| --- | --- |
| Orthogonal | `b1=(1.2,0)`, `b2=(0,0.8)`; signed indices, phase, sky centers, and `j/i` order match. |
| Rotated orthogonal | A 25° basis preserves 1.2° and 0.8° vector lengths and zero dot product. Candidate coordinates remain basis-derived at footprint PA 0° and 60°. |
| Triangular | `b1=(1,0)`, `b2=(0.5,sqrt(3)/2)` yields six unit-distance nearest neighbors and half-step staggered rows with ordinary integer labels. |
| Oblique | `b1=(0.8,0.3)`, `b2=(-0.2,0.9)` has determinant 0.78; signed index `(2,-1)` gives `(1.8,-0.3)` local degrees. Region-local phase `(0.12,-0.17)` places site `(0,0)` accordingly. |
| Footprint cross | Rectangle, circle, and regular six-vertex polygon each use both orthogonal and triangular bases. Their raw 25-site integer domain is identical for a given basis, while candidates and final plans are checked at their separate levels. |
| Origin | A region translated by `(0.25° RA,0.125° DEC)` moves a `region_center` site `(0,0)` by the same stated bounds shift. The fixed anchor stays at `(150°,0°)` across region changes. |
| Projection | Fixed-anchor sites at DEC `0°`, `+30°`, `-45°`, and `+70°` use the anchor cosine for east spacing and add north offsets to DEC. RA crossing 0°/360° wraps consistently. Repeated generations keep indices and order. |
| Boundary completeness | Rectangle, slanted polygon, thin polygon between rows, subcell polygon, and RA-wrap polygon match independent wide-domain enumeration for rectangle, circle, and hexagonal polygon footprints. A separate thin-region fixture retains two centers outside the polygon because their circles overlap it. |

The existing focused inference suite independently covers disabled/manual/legacy
outcomes, absent or insufficient centers/pairs, successful alignment, outliers,
allowed/forbidden rotation, fixed-anchor consistency, RA wrap, and stable
dataset grouping. Its successful fit leaves the declared profile unchanged and
does not change footprint geometry, PA, overlap, dithers, or coverage role.

The corrected S-PLUS planner/direct coverage fixtures remain regression
authority. Registered v2 `legacy_splus` uses inferred/profile lattice sites for
both Complete and Efficient; Complete may finish below 100% sampled coverage
after useful sites are exhausted. Efficient shares candidates and applies its
configured stopping policy. No supplemental phase, site, or dither is added.
The historical `golden.json` is not regenerated.

The explicit high-declination compatibility fixture uses a 0.4° inline v1
rectangle with zero effective overlap in a 0.91° RA by 1.47° DEC region centered
at DEC `+75°`. Its frozen path selects ten tiles from nine primary candidates:
one historical overlap-fill center is outside that primary candidate list.
Registered v2 `legacy_splus` and generic declared lattice plans on the same
region select only their own candidates and report no supplemental fill. This
isolates the v1 behavior without imposing it on other placement models.

## Placement provenance decision

`GenerationMethod` identifies the generation path; `TileRecord.source` separates
original catalogue rows from proposals; `InferenceRole` gates a dataset's use
as inference evidence. Dataset ID, name, original CSV fields, proposal solution,
and inferred indices provide useful current diagnostics. None establishes that
an imported center is scientifically authoritative. A filename or an inferred
regular pattern cannot establish that authority.

The Gate 4 runtime meaning is frozen as five origins: `manual`,
`imported_unverified`, `authoritative_import`, `declared_profile_lattice`, and
`local_inference`. `authoritative_import` requires an explicit user declaration
and named survey/release/product source reference. Inference retains its fit in
diagnostics, not in the declared profile. Existing v2 rows lack this declaration
and remain unverified. Persisting origin and source reference per pointing,
including through catalogue session state and v3 export, belongs to the Gate 6
provenance model and Gate 7 authoring/export UI. Adding a guessed optional field
to v2 rows now would misstate imported authority and risk classifying inline v1
supplemental centers as declared lattice sites. No partial v3 field was added.

## Empirical scope and limitations

The protected empirical placement case is T80/S-PLUS, using the existing
corrected planner/direct coverage contracts and the sourced instrument matrix.
The matrix does not give another selected case an exact survey-wide lattice
fixture. KCWI, MUSE instrument-only modes, CALIFA/MaNGA galaxy centers, PFS,
and 4MOST do not receive invented regular grids. An instrument's hexagonal
footprint is never evidence of triangular placement. The matrix's small S-PLUS
exposure offsets are an observing sequence, not lattice spacing.

This is a local wrapped-RA/cosine approximation, not a global spherical
tessellation. The near-pole cosine clamp and tangent-plane RA branch remain
documented limits. A candidate budget of 1,200 rejects oversized searches;
it does not truncate an otherwise admissible range. Coverage is sampled, so
remaining-gap fractions are estimates, not an exact continuous-area proof.
Valid declared or inferred placement can leave residual uncovered samples.
Gate 4 does not create off-lattice pointings to force 100%.

No mathematical or empirical fixture revealed a production placement defect.
The two-vector basis abstraction is sufficient for the tested orthogonal,
rotated, triangular, and oblique layouts. Gate 5 PA/sequence work and Gate 6
provenance/profile work remain separate.
